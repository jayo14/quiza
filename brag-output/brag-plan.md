# Brag Plan: Quiza

## What is this app?
Quiza is an AI-powered active recall and study platform that turns lecture notes, slides, and textbooks into 100% grounded RAG quizzes, pinpoints learning gaps with AI mistake analysis, and generates targeted practice tests.

## The angle
Passive studying is broken: students spend up to 80% of their study time highlighting textbooks and re-reading notes, retaining almost nothing. Quiza eliminates this friction with a 100% context-grounded RAG engine—zero hallucinations, direct citations, and mathematical weakness detection that tells you exactly what to fix before exam day.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A bold, high-contrast typography statement:
"Students spend 80% of study time passively re-reading."
"Retaining almost nothing."
Followed by the swift arrival of Quiza:
"Stop re-reading. Start active recall."

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Document Ingestion & RAG Indexing:** A PDF document (`neurobiology-exam-prep.pdf`) is dropped in, parsing through multi-stage background pipelines with real-time status pills ("Reading content", "Generating quiz with AI").
2. **Interactive Grounded Quiz:** A realistic multi-choice question appears on screen. The simulated cursor selects option B ("Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)"), instantly verifying with a glowing green badge: "✓ Correct · Grounded Citation: Lecture 4, Slide 18".
3. **Algorithmic Weakness Analysis & Targeted Practice:** The topic breakdown dashboard highlights concept mastery, flags an area of weakness ("Action Potentials · HIGH SEVERITY · 45% Accuracy"), and generates a 1-click personalized practice quiz.

## Outro / punchline
The luminous Quiza icon (Q) and brand title lock in:
"Zero Hallucinations. 100% Grounded Retention."
"Master any subject with AI-powered active recall."
CTA: "Try Quiza Today."

## User flow worth showing
Upload study document (`neurobiology-exam-prep.pdf`) → AI generates grounded question with instant citation verification → Weakness detector flags concept gap and generates targeted practice quiz to achieve mastery.
